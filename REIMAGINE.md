Our suggestions

1. We get a short video from Shilad explaining what we are going to be doing and why it’s important.  
Pros: It gives a less text based intro which reduces reader fatigue which these types of projects often induce. 
Cons: A bit slow, not automated. 

2. Shortening the exercises to bite sized portions so that we can understand it. 
Pros: nailing down the fundamentals and building deeper understanding
Cons: feeling less “productive” because the assignments were shorter.

3. Every 10 minutes of worktime triggers a short summary/recap of what we have done and a roadmap of where we are going, with a few questions asked to the user with the goal of assessing understanding. 
Pros: Developing a greater understanding of the material, and refreshing short-term memory.
Cons: Frequent checks might be annoying for someone who fully understands where he is and wants to just run with it. 


We chose option 3, and wrote a new prototype for testing it. You can see the results in transcript.
